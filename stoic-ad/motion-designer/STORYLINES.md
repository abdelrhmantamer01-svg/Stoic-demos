# Storylines (motion designer ad)

Recommended and built: **A. The dot that won't stay** - a stranger follows it with zero context, and the film proves its own claim.

## A. The dot that won't stay (built)
Device: the voice gives orders to a red dot; the dot disobeys, then does every job a motion designer sells. Form: one carrier through object-to-object transitions.
First 2 s: bone/ink stage, a red dot, "THIS IS A DOT." She says "Dot. Stay." and a 500 px STAY slab drops on it.
Beats: stay (it escapes) -> wakes a dead cafe poster -> gives a still logo a pulse -> shows off an app in 3D -> says it LOUD -> an eye draws: "you've been following a dot this whole time" with the film's real timecode -> "now imagine it's your brand" -> $300 launch offer -> name + WhatsApp, the full stop wanders off.
Look: bone #F1EEE8, ink #141414, one red #E63946 (the dot), Bebas Neue; each world gets its own flat ground. Sound: voice is the clock, effects only on contacts, no music.

## B. The frame argues with a still
A static sneaker poster insists it is "fine as it is" in on-screen subtitles while the voice animates pieces of it against its will. Split-screen that collapses to one. Funny, but needs a second voice to land and leans on an invented brand for the whole film.

## C. One word, seven verbs
Only the word MOVE on screen; the voice lists what a brand needs (launch, explain, sell) and the word performs each as pure kinetic type. Striking, but abstract: no before/after proof and no product a business owner recognises.
