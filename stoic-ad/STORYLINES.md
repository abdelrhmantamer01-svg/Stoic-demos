# Stoic Agency ad: three storylines

Format: vertical 9:16 (TikTok / Reels), 25–30 s, female voiceover + music + sound on every action.
Real material: ProGarage (dark/red) and Sunshine Blooms (light/orange) demo sites, Stoic Agency brand (#E63946 red, Bebas Neue).

## B. "Hi, I'm your website" (RECOMMENDED: the out-of-the-box one)
Device: the website narrates itself, first person. Form: one frame that keeps rebuilding itself, no hard cuts.
- First 2 s: an empty browser, a blinking cursor. Her voice: "Hi. I'm your website… well, I will be."
- Beats: empty browser → "First, I need a name": search bar types `yourbusiness.ae`, domain locks in with a click → "Now let me get dressed": the page builds in 3D layers (nav drops, headline slams, cards fly in from depth) → "I can be bold…" ProGarage → whip-flip → "…or gentle" Sunshine Blooms → pages fan out in 3D (Home / Services / Booking) → "and I fit in your pocket": page folds into a 3D phone → "I usually cost $1,200. This month? $500." price smashes → end card: STOIC AGENCY mark, WhatsApp CTA.
- Look: starts black and empty, every scene borrows the real site's colours, ends on Stoic red.
- Sound: low pulse that grows as the site builds; clicks, whooshes, a cash-register "snap" on the price.

## A. "Not Found" (the safe, sales-heavy one)
Device: a question with the input. Form: endless zoom.
- First 2 s: a search bar types "best garage in abu dhabi". Results fall in; the viewer's business is a grey "not found" row.
- Beats: search → "they found your competitor" → zoom into the search bar → your domain typed → dive through the address bar into the real site building in 3D → second site → phone → price → end card.
- Look: dark, red accent, big Bebas Neue type. Sound: tense tick, then release on the domain click.

## C. "Open 24/7" (the fastest, most TikTok-native one)
Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.
- First 2 s: giant word "CLOSED." on a shop sign, flips to "OPEN 24/7" = your website.
- Beats: kinetic words on the beat (YOUR NAME / YOUR DOMAIN / EVERY PHONE), real site fragments flash behind each, price slam, end card.
- Look: Stoic red and black, full-frame type. Sound: punchy beat, hard cuts on the kick.
