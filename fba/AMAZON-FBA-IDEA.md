# Amazon FBA idea: custom-fit windshield sunshades for the Gulf's best-selling SUVs

**Product:** a reflective, foldable windshield sunshade cut to the exact windshield of the UAE's top models
(launch: Nissan Patrol, Toyota Land Cruiser, Toyota Hilux), sold on Amazon.ae through FBA.

## Why this one
- **Real, local, painful need:** 50°C summers; every parked car bakes. Not a fad.
- **People already pay double for fit:** custom-fit shades sell at AED 130–222 on UAE stores vs AED 57–69 for universal ones
  (a Proadsy custom-fit model shows 19,198 reviews).
- **Gap:** the custom-fit sellers cover many US/Japanese sedans; the Gulf's big SUVs are a smaller, under-served slice
  where "Patrol 2025 sunshade" is a search a generic seller loses.
- **Your edge:** mechanical engineering (fit, materials, measured heat reduction) + motion design (the listing video
  and an Instagram/TikTok "dashboard 78°C → 45°C" test video that generic sellers don't make).

## Start-up cost (rough)
UAE trade licence with e-commerce activity (free zone, roughly AED 6–15k a year) + Amazon Professional plan +
first 500 units (~AED 17.5k landed) + samples. Budget ≈ AED 35–45k. Product photos and video: you make them.

## Risks (honest)
- **You need a UAE trade licence** to sell on Amazon.ae, now actively enforced; that's the biggest fixed cost for a student.
- **Competitors exist** (Proadsy, Arismotor, EzyShade). You win only on exact Gulf-SUV fit, Arabic + English listing, and proof videos.
- **Seasonal:** sales peak April–October. Launch inventory must land by March.
- **Fit risk:** a wrong template = returns. Get a sample per model and test it on a real car before ordering 500.
