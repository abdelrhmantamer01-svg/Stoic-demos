Read the reference that matches the job:

| Job | Read |
|---|---|
| Find prospects, trigger events, Google dorks | `references/prospecting_tactics.md`, `references/signal_playbook.md` |

Rules that always apply: only contact real, verified business contacts found on public pages; never guess facts about a prospect; short, plain emails with one clear ask; honour every opt-out immediately; nothing is sent without the user's explicit approval of that exact message.
